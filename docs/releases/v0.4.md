- Repair: parts touching along a face (a plate against a box with the same outline) stay whole, separate bodies instead of losing triangles; small parts standing on a bigger one (studs on a plate) are kept and closed instead of removed as scraps.
- "Sharpen roundings": small fillets and chamfers become sharp edges before recognition (for FDM prints); holes and pins that go all round are still recognised as cylinders, however small.
- Faster recognition of large meshes (plane steps no longer compare everything with everything); selections can be undone.
- The converter view runs on the desktop and, as a trial, in a browser.
